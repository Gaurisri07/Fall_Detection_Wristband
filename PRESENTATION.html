📡 GPS — How it works

1
Serial2 receives raw NMEA bytes from NEO-6M chip (GPIO 16)
2
Characters assembled into sentences starting with $
3
XOR checksum validated on each sentence
4
$GPRMC → lat, lon, speed, course, time, date, fix status
5
$GPGGA → satellites, HDOP, altitude
6
Fix debounced for 500 ms before declared stable
7
Timeout after 35 s if no fix (handles cold-start failure)
