LIKELY QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
────────────────────────────

Q: Why didn't you use TinyGPS++ or a library?
A: "I wrote a manual parser so there are zero library dependencies in
   the Wokwi simulation. The algorithm is also cleaner to demonstrate
   and debug. TinyGPS++ is a direct drop-in upgrade for Phase 2 if needed."

Q: What happens if there's no GPS fix?
A: "I handle it explicitly — the SMS still sends, but the location field
   says 'NO FIX' instead of crashing or sending 0,0. There's also a
   35-second timeout with a flag the state machine can check."

Q: How does Person B use your module?
A: "They include my two header files and call four functions:
   gps_init(), gsm_init() in setup, gps_update() and gsm_update()
   every loop, and gsm_send_alert() when a fall is confirmed.
   They don't touch anything else in /comms/."

Q: How do you go from simulation to real hardware?
A: "Change one line: #define GSM_STUB_MODE from 1 to 0.
   The SIM800L AT command driver is already written and sitting in
   gsm_module.cpp, just disabled by that flag."
